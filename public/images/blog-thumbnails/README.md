# Blog Thumbnails

Place thumbnail images for your blog posts here. Each image should correspond to a blog post.

## Naming Convention

Use descriptive, lowercase filenames with hyphens matching the blog post titles:

- `finnish-learning.jpg` - For "Is it possible to learn Finnish fluently in less than a year?"
- `unicorn-startup.jpg` - For "What does it really take to build a unicorn?"
- `building-opportunities.jpg` - For "From no job, no money, no network..."
- `linkedin-platform.jpg` - For "LinkedIn is my favorite professional platform..."
- `finnish-integration.jpg` - For "From Marie Curie Postdoc Fellow to Night-Shift Worker..."
- `communities-finland.jpg` - For "Are you an international professional in Finland..."
- `volunteering-finland.jpg` - For "Looking for volunteering opportunities..."
- `recruitment-events.jpg` - For "Are you looking to advance your career..."
- `mentoring-programs.jpg` - For "Looking to advance your career or give back..."
- `research-tools.jpg` - For "Essential Tools, Resources, and Academies..."
- `startup-programs.jpg` - For "Aspiring entrepreneur or startup founder..."
- `linkedin-ssi.jpg` - For "Proud to share my LinkedIn Social Selling Index..."
- `hive-helsinki.jpg` - For "Spotlight on Hive Helsinki..."
- `suomen-mestari.jpg` - For "Unforgettable Opportunity: Meeting the Co-Author..."
- `job-portals.jpg` - For "Discover Top Job Portals and Websites..."
- `hidden-job-market.jpg` - For "Unlocking the Hidden Job Market..."

## Image Specifications

- **Recommended size**: 400x400px or 600x400px (aspect ratio 1:1 or 3:2)
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **File size**: Keep under 500KB for optimal performance
- **Quality**: High quality but optimized for web

## Supported Formats

- `.jpg` / `.jpeg`
- `.png`
- `.webp`

The images will automatically be optimized by Next.js Image component.
